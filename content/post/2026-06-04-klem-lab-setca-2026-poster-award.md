Dr. Arkanil Roy and Young Woo Kim won a poster award sponsored by Digital Discovery for their poster, “QMzyme: A Python Toolkit to Facilitate QM-Based Active Site Modeling.”

Their work highlights how QMzyme streamlines the construction of quantum mechanical enzyme active site models and supports reproducible modeling workflows for computational enzymology.
