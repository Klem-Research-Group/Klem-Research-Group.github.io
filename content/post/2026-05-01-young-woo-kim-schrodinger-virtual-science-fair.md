Young Woo Kim, a Ph.D. student in the Klem Research Group, is a winner of the 2026 Schrödinger Molecules and Models Virtual Science Fair. His presentation, “QMzyme: Taming the Complexity of QM-Based Enzyme Modeling.” This recognition highlights the Klem Lab’s ongoing efforts to build tools and workflows that bridge fundamental enzymology with practical computational research. Congratulations to Young Woo on this outstanding achievement!
