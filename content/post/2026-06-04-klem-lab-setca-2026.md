The Klem Research Group attended the 2026 SETCA meeting in Atlanta, Georgia. Heidi Klem was invited to give a talk titled “Modeling the Enzyme Active Site: A Moving Target in Mechanistic Chemistry.”

Her presentation highlighted the challenges of defining QM-based enzyme active sites and the role of computational models in resolving mechanistic chemistry questions.
